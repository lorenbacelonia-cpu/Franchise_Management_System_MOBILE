How It Works

Official 6-Step Verification Guide

Step 1

Open App & Sign In

Launch the app and log in with your Passenger or TRD Traffic Enforcer account.

Step 2

Allow Camera Access

Grant camera permission when prompted to enable real-time QR code scanning.

Step 3

Select Vehicle Type

Toggle between Pedicab or Tricycle mode using the switch button to match the vehicle.

Step 4

Scan Official QR Code

Align the camera reticle steadily with the official TRD QR code on the vehicle.

Step 5

Verify Franchise Record

Check operator, driver name, City Plate no., Parada / Route, and franchise validity.

Step 6 Optional

Submit Report / Issue Citation

Report overcharging directly to TRD, or issue citations if logged in as an Enforcer.
